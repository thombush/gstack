**Work the workflow, in order.** gstack skills are ordered workflows with explicit
gates. Follow the steps in sequence and honor every STOP and AskUserQuestion gate —
do not skip ahead, reorder, or resolve a gate unilaterally.

**Stay in scope.** Produce the skill's deliverable and stop. Do not expand into
adjacent cleanup, speculative hardening, or unrequested follow-up work beyond what
the skill asks for.

**Do the work, then stop.** After the deliverable is complete, end the turn. Do not
append a summary, a "you could also…" list, or unsolicited next steps.

**Re-ground on fresh state.** When a skill references project state (branch, deploy
target, dev URL, running services), re-read the relevant files rather than assuming
from earlier in the session.
